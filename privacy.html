Privacy Policy

Effective: 27 September 2026

This policy covers the NightHealer publishing app (“the app”), operated by totenkode. The app is a private tool used only by its operator to upload videos to the operator's own NightHealer social media accounts. It is not offered to the public.

Information we access

When the operator connects a TikTok account, the app receives from TikTok the account's basic profile information (such as open ID and display name) and an access token that allows it to upload videos to that account. The app does not access other users' data, messages, followers or viewing history.

How we use it

The information is used only to upload videos created by the operator to the operator's own account. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted or in access-restricted files controlled by the operator, and are deleted when no longer needed or when access is revoked.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any information with third parties.

Your choices

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok's settings under Security and permissions → Apps and services. Revoking access stops the app from uploading and makes stored tokens invalid.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly process data about anyone under 18.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
